Notto 1.7.2

Fixed
• Notes restored from another app are visible again.
  A backup made elsewhere usually has no card colour, and a card without a
  colour draws no background — so in dark mode the text sat on the dark page
  and the notes looked empty. Restored notes now get a valid card colour.
• Every note is checked for the fields the app needs (colour, title, checklist
  items, timestamp) when it is restored and when the app opens, so notes already
  restored are repaired on launch — no need to restore again.
