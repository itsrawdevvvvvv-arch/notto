Notto 1.9.0

New
• Text-size slider in the keyboard's Format tab — any size from 12 to 34,
  instead of just 14/16/18. Saved per note.
• Text case changer — ABC (UPPERCASE), abc (lowercase), Abc (Title Case),
  Abc. (Sentence case). Changes just your selection, or the whole note.

Fixed
• The slider now drags smoothly and the keyboard stays open while you use it.
