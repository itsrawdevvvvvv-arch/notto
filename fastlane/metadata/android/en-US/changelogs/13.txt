Notto 1.8.0

New
• Calendar — find it in the + menu.
  A month view in the app's own style: tap any day to mark it done (it fills
  and gets a line through it), tap again to unmark. The header shows how many
  days you've marked, with a progress bar that fills as you go. Arrows move
  between months; the calendar badge jumps back to today.
• Marks are saved on your device.

Fixed
• The Calendar, Clock and Group screens now follow the light theme.
  They were stuck on dark even when the app was in light mode.
