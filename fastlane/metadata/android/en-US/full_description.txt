Notto is a fast, private, offline notes app. Write notes, build checklists, arrange moodboards, doodle and dictate — all in one dark, tactile interface. Everything stays on your device: no account, no tracking, no internet permission.

NOTES
Rich text with bold, italic, underline, highlights, bullet and numbered lists, custom sizes, colours and fonts.

CHECKLISTS
Tap to tick items off. Home cards show your progress at a glance.

IMAGE NOTES & MOODBOARDS
Add photos, stickers and text, then drag, rotate, scale and layer them on a board.

SKETCH
A smooth free-hand canvas you can drop straight into a note.

VOICE NOTES
Dictate hands-free and turn speech into a note.

AI IMPORT
Copy a ready-made prompt into any AI tool, paste the answer back and Notto formats it for you.

EXPORT & SHARE
Save any note as PNG, PDF or TXT.

ORGANISE
Pin important notes, filter by type, and sort by recency.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No analytics, no ads, no network access. Your notes live in local storage on your device.

SMALL & FAST
One lightweight APK, no third-party runtime, works fully offline.
