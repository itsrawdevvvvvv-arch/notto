# Privacy Policy — Notto

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

Notto is designed to be private by default. This policy explains, plainly, what
the app does with your information: nothing leaves your device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Notto has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no
user accounts. The app does not transmit any data anywhere.

## Where your notes are stored

Your notes, checklists, images, sketches and settings are stored **locally on
your device**, in the app's own storage (WebView local storage and IndexedDB).
They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Network access

Notto requests **no `INTERNET` permission**. It cannot make network requests
even if it wanted to. All fonts and code needed by the app are bundled inside
the APK, so it works completely offline.

## Permissions

Notto requests no runtime permissions. When you add a photo or import a file,
Android's system file picker asks you to choose a file — Notto only ever
receives the specific file you pick.

## Exports

When you export a note as PNG, PDF or TXT, the file is written to your device's
Downloads folder (or app storage on older Android versions). It stays on your
device unless you choose to share it.

## Children

Notto does not collect any data and is safe for all ages.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will be published in this repository.
